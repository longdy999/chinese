How to Get the Most From This Plan

A few evidence-based habits that make 30 focused minutes go a long way. គន្លឹះមួយចំនួនដើម្បីប្រើ ៣០ នាទីឱ្យមានប្រសិទ្ធភាព។

🎯 Protect the Core 30 minutes
Treat your daily session like a meeting you can't cancel. Same time, same place. Showing up daily matters more than any single long session.
ចាត់ទុក ៣០ នាទីប្រចាំថ្ងៃជាការប្រជុំដែលលុបចោលមិនបាន។
🔁 Use spaced repetition
Add each day's words to a flashcard app (Anki, Pleco). Reviewing words just before you forget them is the most efficient way to build long-term memory.
បន្ថែមពាក្យទៅកម្មវិធី flashcard ហើយនមុនពេលអ្នកភ្លេច។
🗣️ Speak from day one
Don't wait until you feel ready. Say every word out loud, even imperfectly. Production — not just recognition — is what builds real fluency.
និយាយឱ្យឮតាំងពីថ្ងៃដំបូង សូម្បីតែមិនល្អក៏ដោយ។
👂 Make input comprehensible
Choose listening material slightly above your level on topics you already understand (your own field). Familiar context lets you absorb new words faster.
ជ្រើសសម្ភារៈស្តាប់ខ្ពស់ជាងកម្រិតបន្តិច ស្តីពីវិស័យដែលអ្នកស្គាល់។
✍️ Write to remember
Handwriting characters and drafting short sentences engages memory more deeply than passive reading. The Growth track's writing block is worth the 7 minutes.
ការសរសេរអក្សរ និងប្រយោគខ្លីៗ ជួយចងចាំបានជ្រៅជាងការអានស្ងៀមៗ។
📊 Apply it at work
Whenever possible, use a new word the same day in a real message, note, or conversation. Application turns vocabulary into a skill you own.
ប្រើពាក្យថ្មីនៅកន្លែងធ្វើការនៅថ្ងៃតែមួយ ដើម្បីប្តូរវាក្យសព្ទទៅជាជំនាញ។
🧭 Review beats rushing
If a week's checkpoint isn't met, repeat instead of pushing ahead. A solid foundation makes every later week easier.
បើមិនទាន់ឆ្លងកាត់ checkpoint សូមធ្វើឡើងវិញ ជាជាងប្រញាប់ទៅមុខ។
📈 Track your streak
The stats bar saves your progress automatically. Watching your streak and word count grow is a simple, powerful motivator.
របារស្ថិតិរក្សាទុកវឌ្ឍនភាពដោយស្វ័យប្រវត្តិ — តាមដានដើម្បីលើកទឹកចិត្ត។
