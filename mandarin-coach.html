Hello & Nice to Meet You

30 min
▼
📚
Vocabulary
10 min
您好
Nín hǎo
Hello (formal/respectful)
ជំរាបសួរ (ផ្លូវការ)
您好，很高兴认识您。
Hello, nice to meet you.
我叫
Wǒ jiào
My name is...
ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ...
我叫 [your name]。
My name is [your name].
很高兴
Hěn gāoxìng
Very pleased / Happy to
រីករាយណាស់
很高兴认识您！
Very pleased to meet you!
📖
Grammar Focus
8 min
Pattern: Subject + 是 (shì) + Noun
我是 [job/company]。
Wǒ shì [job/company]. = I am [job/company].
ខ្ញុំជា... — ប្រើ 是 (shì) ដើម្បីពណ៌នាអ្វីដែលអ្នកជា
Example: 我是经理。(Wǒ shì jīnglǐ.) = I am a manager.
🎤
Speaking Practice
7 min
Say out loud 5 times: "您好，我叫___，很高兴认识您！"
និយាយឱ្យបានដង ៥: ជំរាបសួរ ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះ___ រីករាយណាស់ដែលបានស្គាល់
Nín hǎo, wǒ jiào ___, hěn gāoxìng rènshi nín!
Practice bowing slightly as you say 您好 — body language matters in Chinese business culture.
ហ្វឹកហ្វឺនការឱ្យដងក ខណៈពេលនិយាយ 您好 — ភាសាកាយវិការសំខាន់ក្នុងវប្បធម៌ជំនួញចិន
👂
Listening Task
5 min
Search YouTube: "Chinese business greeting 您好" — listen to 3 short clips and repeat what you hear.
ស្វែងរកនៅ YouTube: "Chinese business greeting 您好" — ស្តាប់ clip ខ្លីៗ ៣ ហើយនិយាយតាម

Culture Note
5 min
🏮 Business Card Etiquette
Always give and receive cards with both hands. Look at it carefully before putting it away — never write on it or stuff it in your pocket. Say 请多关照 when handing yours over.
ផ្តល់និងទទួលកាតដោយដៃទាំងពីរ — ការសំខាន់ណាស់ក្នុងជំនួញចិន

📖
5 Key Business Culture Rules
15 min
🏮 Essential Chinese Business Culture
1. 面子 (miànzi) — "Face"/Reputation: Never embarrass someone publicly. Always let them save face.
2. 关系 (guānxi) — Relationships first, business second.
3. Seniority — Greet the most senior person first. Use 您 (nín) not 你 (nǐ).
4. Patience — Decisions take time. Don't rush 考虑一下 (let me think).
5. Gifts — Give with both hands. Avoid clocks (送终 = death omen) and green hats.
វប្បធម៌ជំនួញចិន ៥ ចំណុចសំខាន់: មុខ, ទំនាក់ទំនង, ចំណាត់ថ្នាក់, ការអត់ធ្មត់, អំណោយ

Study Tips & Strategy

学习技巧 — Maximize your 30 minutes per day

🎯 The 80/20 Rule for Business Mandarin
You don't need 5,000 words. Focus on the 200–300 most-used business phrases and master them deeply. Speaking 50 phrases with perfect confidence beats knowing 500 haphazardly. This plan gives you exactly those 200+ high-impact phrases.
អ្នកមិនចាំបាច់ពាក្យ ៥,០០០ ទេ — ផ្ដោតលើ ២០០ ឃ្លាជំនួញ និយាយបានច្បាស់ ១០០ ខ្លាំងជាងស្គាល់ ៥០០ ខ្លួន
🔁 Spaced Repetition
Review yesterday's vocabulary at the start of each session (5 min). Re-read Week 1 phrases on Week 3. Your brain retains vocabulary best when it's reviewed at increasing intervals: 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 30 days.
ពិនិត្យពាក្យកាលពីម្សិលមិញ នៅដើមរៀននីមួយៗ — ខ្លួនរបស់អ្នករក្សាពាក្យបានល្អបំផុតតាមរយៈការពិនិត្យម្ដងៗ
🎤 Say It Out Loud — Always
Mandarin tones are MUSCULAR memory, not just mental. You must physically speak the words to train your mouth and ear. Whisper if you're in public. Never just read silently — always vocalize.
ត្រូវតែនិយាយបញ្ចេញជាសំឡេង — សំឡេងភាសាចិនជាការចងចាំសាច់ដុំ មិនមែនត្រឹមចិត្ត

🏮 The Secret Weapon: Start in Chinese, Finish in English
In real meetings, open with your first 3 sentences in Mandarin (greeting + intro + 请多关照). Then say "我的中文不太好，我们可以用英语吗？" Chinese clients will be so impressed they'll be warm for the entire meeting — even conducted in English.
ចាប់ផ្ដើមប្រជុំជំនួញជាភាសាចិន ៣ ប្រយោគ — ក្រោយមកសុំប្ដូរទៅអង់គ្លេស — ការបង្ហាញនេះបង្កើតទំនាក់ទំនងបានរហ័ស!
📊 Week-by-Week Strategy
Week 1: Nail your introduction cold. Practice until it's automatic.
Week 2: Learn to negotiate. Money phrases are the most valuable.
Week 3: Focus on relationship-building language. Guānxi wins deals.
Week 4: Put it all together. Simulate real client scenarios daily.

🎯 30-Day Goal Assessment
After 30 days you should be able to: introduce yourself professionally, schedule a meeting, discuss pricing, handle basic objections, give a business dinner toast, write a WeChat follow-up, and recover gracefully when you don't understand. That's enough for REAL client interactions.
